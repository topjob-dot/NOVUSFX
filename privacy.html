1. Who we are

NovusFX Markets (“NovusFX”, “we”, “us”) is operated by [Company legal name], [registered address]. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. Our regulatory license application is in progress; we will state our licensing status here once it is approved.

Questions about this policy or your data: [privacy@yourdomain.com].

2. What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, country, and password (stored only as a secure hash by our login provider).
  • Verification details: identity and address documents we need to verify who you are and to meet anti-money-laundering rules.
  • Security details: two-factor authentication settings, login times, and the devices and IP addresses used to sign in.
  • Payment details: the mobile money number or crypto wallet address you fund from, amounts, references, timestamps, and the status of each deposit and withdrawal. We never see your M-Pesa or Airtel Money PIN.
  • Trading activity: orders, positions, balances and account history.
  • Support messages: what you type into the chat assistant or send to our support desk.
  • Technical data: browser type, device, pages visited and cookies (see Cookies below).

3. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account, and to run 2FA.
  • To process deposits, withdrawals and trades, and to show them on your dashboard.
  • To verify your identity and prevent fraud, money laundering and other misuse.
  • To answer your questions and provide support.
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations.
  • To improve the platform, fix errors and understand how the site is used.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Our lawful bases

Under Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019 we process your data because: it is needed to provide the service you asked for (contract); we are required to by law, such as identity checks and record keeping; we have a legitimate interest in keeping the platform secure and preventing fraud; or you have given consent, for example for marketing messages. Where we rely on consent you can withdraw it at any time.

5. Who we share it with

We share data only where needed to run the service:

  • Infrastructure providers that host our login, database and functions (Google Firebase / Google Cloud).
  • Payment providers that process M-Pesa, Airtel Money and crypto deposits and withdrawals.
  • Identity verification and market-data providers, where we use them.
  • Regulators, courts and law enforcement when we are legally required to.
  • Professional advisers such as auditors and lawyers, under confidentiality.

Each provider may only use the data to perform its service for us.

6. International transfers

Some providers store or process data outside Kenya. When we transfer personal data abroad we make sure the destination offers appropriate safeguards, as the Data Protection Act requires.

7. How long we keep it

We keep account and transaction records for as long as your account is open and afterwards for [retention period, e.g. seven years], because financial and anti-money-laundering rules require it. Support chats and technical logs are kept for [period, e.g. 12 months]. When a retention period ends we delete or anonymise the data.

8. How we protect it

Accounts are protected by mandatory two-factor authentication. Data is encrypted in transit, access to it is limited to staff who need it, and payment credentials are handled by our payment providers. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and never share your codes. If a breach puts your rights at risk we will notify you and the Data Commissioner as the law requires.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to: tell you what data we hold and give you a copy; correct data that is wrong; delete data we no longer need to keep; restrict or object to certain processing; and give you your data in a portable format. You can also object to direct marketing at any time. Email [privacy@yourdomain.com] and we will reply within the time the law sets. Some records cannot be deleted while a legal retention duty applies.

If you are unhappy with our answer you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) in Kenya.

10. Cookies

We use essential cookies and local storage to keep you signed in and remember your settings. If we add analytics or advertising cookies we will ask for your consent first, and you can also block cookies in your browser settings, though the dashboard may not work without the essential ones.

11. Chat assistant

The on-site assistant gives automated answers to common questions. Please do not type passwords, PINs, 2FA codes or full card or wallet secrets into it. Messages may be stored to improve support.

12. Age limit

NovusFX is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18 and will delete it if we find we have.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of the page and, for significant changes, tell you by email or on your dashboard.
